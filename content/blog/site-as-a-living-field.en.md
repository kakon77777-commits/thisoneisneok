# Rebuilding a Personal Site as a Living Field

I have recently reorganized the roles of my websites.

This site does not need to become another enormous paper repository. Logic Matrix already carries the full theory corpus, timeline, and large collection of nodes. Moving all of that into a personal site again would only multiply card management, classification, translation, and version work.

> Reducing management overhead does not mean reducing intellectual density. It means returning each kind of content to the container where it actually belongs.

## What remains here

The rebuilt personal site keeps only a few kinds of content that will continue to be used:

- a personal blog and daily written records;
- applications and experiments that can be operated or demonstrated;
- MSSP tutorials, project archaeology, and reference implementations;
- Lean4 verification and only the companion papers required to understand it;
- a small selection of books and older works still worth presenting.

The site no longer needs to pretend that it is a completed universal archive. It becomes a personal field that can change, be corrected, and preserve visible version traces.

## Markdown is the source; HTML and PDF are human interfaces

Each article is preserved as Markdown. From that single source, the site generates:

1. an HTML page for everyday reading;
2. a PDF for preservation and printing;
3. an absolute URL for the raw Markdown;
4. a structured index for AI readers.

Humans do not have to read raw text files, and AI systems do not have to reverse-engineer structure from a decorative page.

## Version is no longer maturity

In an age where AI can continually inspect, rewrite, and extend a text, “maturity” may simply be the name we give to a temporary pause in revision.

This site therefore emphasizes versions, update dates, and lifecycle states rather than claiming final completion. `Evolving`, `Prototype`, and `Semi-stable` often describe reality better than a single mature/immature division.

From today, the site is no longer only a showcase. It is a new entrance for recording.
