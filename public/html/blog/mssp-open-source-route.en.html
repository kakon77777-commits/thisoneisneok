MSSP's First Public Route: Starting from Open Source

If MSSP remains only an abstract architecture, it can easily become a vocabulary that only its designer understands.

The first useful public route is therefore not to add more definitions. It is to keep finding open-source projects and identify their state, events, coupling, and replacement boundaries in practice.

Comparison matters more than promotion

Every reconstruction should preserve at least three views:

  • a structural map of the original project;
  • the MSSP-oriented comparison version;
  • failures and counterexamples that resist clean reconstruction.

This allows MSSP to face comparisons in implementation, performance, legibility, and maintenance cost instead of merely claiming to look cleaner.

Teaching is itself a form of verification

If a method can only be operated by its original author, it has not yet become a transmissible method.

Tutorials, state diagrams, source examples, and version records are therefore not accessories. They are part of whether MSSP can be understood and reused.

The field lab will begin with small projects, then move toward game runtimes, visual state editors, and continuous operation systems for autonomous agents.
